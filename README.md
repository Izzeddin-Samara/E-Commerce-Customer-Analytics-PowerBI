# 📊 E-Commerce Executive Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end Data Analytics project utilizing **Power BI**, **DAX** and **Power Query** to analyze **$7.8M in revenue** and **51K+ orders** across a global customer base. 

This interactive 3-page executive dashboard delivers strategic data insights on customer behavior, sales performance, category profitability, and delivery fulfillment.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Business Intelligence:** Microsoft Power BI (PL-300 Standards)
* **Calculations & Logic:** Data Analysis Expressions (DAX Measures)
* **Data Preparation:** Power Query
* **Data Modeling:** Star Schema Design (Fact & Dimension Tables)
* **Reporting:** Interactive Visualizations and Custom Cards

## 📊 Key KPIs & Data Insights

### 1️⃣ Customer Segmentation & Access Channels
* **Device Dominance:** Web browsing accounts for **92.8% of access** (48K orders), compared to **7.2% via Mobile** (4K orders).
* **Customer Base:** Reached **39K unique customers** generating **51K total orders**.
* **Account Type:** Registered "Member" logins drive the vast majority of sessions compared to Guest, First SignUp, or New sessions.
* **Gender Breakdown:** Balanced distribution with **53.6% Male** and **46.4% Female** shoppers.

### 2️⃣ Sales Trends, Product Performance & Profitability
* **Revenue & Volume:** Analyzed **$7.8M in Total Revenue** across multiple sales categories.
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** **Fashion** leads as the top category revenue generator, supported by Home & Furniture, Auto & Accessories, and Electronics.
* **Unit Profitability:** **Apple Laptops** generate the highest profit margin at **$67/unit**, followed closely by **Tyres at $65/unit**.
* **Seasonality Trends:** Peak revenue occurs in **Q4 (November & December)**, scaling up to over **$400K/month** in sales.

### 3️⃣ Delivery Speed & Order Fulfillment
* **Delivery Timeline:** Average delivery time stands at **5 Days**.
* **Priority Tracking:** Delivery duration is monitored across **Critical, High, Medium, and Low** order priorities.

## 💡 Strategic & Business Recommendations

1. **Digital Optimization:** Prioritize Web UX/UI enhancements given that **92.8%** of customers convert through web channels.
2. **Payment Gateway Integration:** Ensure high uptime and smooth checkouts for Credit Card gateways (primary payment method).
3. **Inventory & Q4 Planning:** Scale inventory holding and launch targeted marketing campaigns ahead of the **Q4 seasonal surge** (Nov–Dec).
4. **Mobile Conversion Strategy:** Develop targeted mobile app incentives to capitalize on the underutilized mobile channel (7.2%).
